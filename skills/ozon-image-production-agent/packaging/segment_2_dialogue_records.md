## 🛠️ Case Study 1: 2C53T 3-in-1 Oscilloscope
* **User Input**:
  > Osciloscop digital portabil cu multimetru si generator de semnal DDS, 2C53T, 3 in 1, Acumulator Li-Ion 3000mAh, Type-C, Iesire 50kHz si un pas de 1Hz, LCD 2, 8 Inch, 19999 de conturi, Latime de banda 50MHz, Rata de esantionare 250MS/s, Albastru
* **Category Routing**: Tech / Electronics -> **Black + Green** Family with cyan accent (`#00e5ff`).
* **Visual Blueprint & Staging**: Standing on a professional electronics workbench with blurred circuit boards and soldering stations in a shallow depth-of-field blur. Red and black probe cables loop dynamically, emitting cyan electric sparks.
* **Layout Overlays**:
  - Top-Left Title: `OSCILLOSCOPE` + `50 MHz` primary badge.
  - Right Column: `3000 mAh`, `250 MS/s`, `Type-C` rounded badges.
  - Circular trust badge: `1-Year Warranty` (cyan circle, no shield).
  - Brand branding: Slanted top-right carbon-fiber **`EXCITAT` Brand Shelf** with glowing neon cyan lighting strips.
* **Fidelity Resolution**: Local alpha matting isolates the exact button, jack, and bumper layout of the blue oscilloscope, while the generator only renders probe wires and the workspace backdrop.
