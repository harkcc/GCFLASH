# Excitat / EXITE Frame Manual Review

Date: 2026-05-22

This review was done by opening the 41 first main images one by one at their
original resolution. Contact sheets are only used as navigation and are not the
source of the conclusions.

## Main Finding

The reference frame system is not a single border. It is a brand sleeve system:

- a large top-right slanted brand tab or brand wordmark
- thin product/category-colored outer strokes
- one or two expressive corners
- occasional paper/floral/tech-line texture in the border
- optional bottom strip for title/specs
- color and decoration follow the product category

Our previous heavy Ozon neon frame is a different language. For EXITE we need a
lighter art-paper brand frame first, then layer Ozon/WB selling energy inside
the card only when the category needs it.

## Stable EXITE Frame Rules

### Required

- Brand must read as `EXITE`, not generic tech text.
- Main brand position: top-right.
- Brand shape: large slanted/italic wordmark with drop shadow.
- The top-right mark should sit on a paper-cut shard or colored tab.
- Outer frame should be thin or medium; avoid thick neon unless the category is gaming/toy tech.
- Color should follow category/product: teal, orange, green, blue, pink, taupe, red, black/gold.

### Optional By Category

- Home/baby/clean appliance: teal or mint paper sleeve, white inner panel, bottom title strip.
- Tools/hardware: orange/red top band, black or gray bottom spec strip.
- Gaming/tech: dark neon interior, top-right logo, but still keep the brand tab geometry.
- Beauty/fashion: pink/mint paper with pearls, bows, soft ornament lines.
- Craft/puzzle/gift: black/pink ornamental border and white inner canvas.
- Premium/office: taupe/black/gold minimal sleeve.

### Avoid

- full-card thick cyber frame for every product
- tiny top-right logo
- border color unrelated to product
- generic rounded label that does not look like the Excitat shard
- letting the frame overpower the product
