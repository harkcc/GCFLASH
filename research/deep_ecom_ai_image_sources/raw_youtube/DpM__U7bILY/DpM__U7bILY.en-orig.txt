And here's an example of an ad campaign where the brand insisted on building a real set to show their product underwater surrounded by the cichlid fish to create this realistic deep blue ocean look with the sunlight beaming through all in real time. Now, you know, typically for a commercial campaign, we create one to two highly detailed product photos a day. often shooting in plates to showcase the product in its best light. But this particular project took a different approach. Here we aim to capture everything in a single in camera shot by building an underwater set in real time. No composits staying true to life. To enhance realism, the bottle was shot underwater in a very special customuilt optically wide glass tank to avoid any grain tint. We also chose vertical dimensions for the glass tank to create photos in both 4x5 and 9x6 aspect ratios for the digital campaign straight in camera. I opted for continuous lighting over flash, mimicking how our eyes perceive sunlight in the real world. Now, we also chose not to retouch certain imperfections on the product to maintain its authenticity. Here's the result. Let me know your thoughts in the comments and I'll see you all in the next one.
