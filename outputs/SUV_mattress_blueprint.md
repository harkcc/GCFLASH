# Universal E-commerce Blueprint: Inflatable SUV Air Mattress

This blueprint documents the design schema, visual layout planning, and staging parameters for the Morovan Auto Inflatable Mattress, following the **EXCITAT Universal Brand Shelf Framework**.

---

## Part 1: Product Analysis & Routing

### 1. Product Attributes
* **Product Name**: Morovan Auto Inflatable Car Mattress (Saltea Auto Gonflabila)
* **Features**: 2-in-1 Travel Bed, 2 Pillows Included, Electric Air Pump & Nozzles, Carry Bag.
* **Compatibility**: SUV, MPV.
* **Dimensions**: 175x130 cm.
* **Color**: Grey (Gri).

### 2. Category Routing (Home & Outdoors / Auto Comfort)
* **Frame Family**: **Teal Paper Sleeve / Outdoors** hybrid.
* **Accent Palette**: Vibrant Teal/Cyan (`#00e5ff`) + Soft Grey base.
* **Staging Context**: The rear interior boot of an SUV/MPV Masina (fully scene-relevant, realistic installation).

### 3. Layout Mapping (English)
* **Brand Wordmark (Top-Right)**: Bold white **`EXCITAT`** speed-lightning wordmark on a slanted teal/graphite paper shard shelf (slanted at `-6°`).
* **Title (Top-Left)**: `CAR AIR MATTRESS`
* **Subtitle**: `2-in-1 SUV Travel Bed`
* **Core Numeric Badge (Click Catch)**: **175 x 130 cm** (Size, inside a rounded cyan badge, top-left).
* **Secondary Feature Badges (Right column)**:
  1. `2 Pillows` (📦 Inflatable pillows)
  2. `Air Pump` (⚡ 12V Electric pump included)
  3. `Carry Bag` (👜 Portable storage)
* **Branded Trust Badge (Right Side)**:
  * A cyan circular badge containing: **`100% Fit`** (large "100%" in center, "SUV / MPV" below).
* **Bottom Accessory Band**: Flat-lay icons of the electric pump, nozzles, pillows, and bag arranged neatly at the bottom with thin white dividers.
