# Universal E-commerce Blueprint: Electric Solar System Assembly Kit

This blueprint documents the design layout planning and staging parameters for the EXCITAT Electric Solar System Assembly Kit, following the **EXCITAT Universal Brand Shelf Framework**.

---

## Part 1: Product Analysis & Routing

### 1. Product Attributes
* **Product Name**: Electric Solar System Assembly Kit
* **Features**: Rotatable planetary orbits, Dual modes (Electric & Manual), Mechanical gear drive, STEM educational gift.
* **Piece Count**: 1098 Pieces.
* **Aesthetics**: Brass-toned gears, glowing solar core (Sun), colorful detailed planets, octagonal base with astronomical markings.

### 2. Category Routing (STEM Toy / Creative Kit)
* **Frame Family**: **Cosmic Science / Study Ambient** (Educational & High-tech).
* **Accent Palette**: Solar Orange / Nebula Gold (`#ff9f00`) + Deep Space Blue/Black.
* **Staging Context**: Placed on a high-quality dark oak study desk, with an out-of-focus background of a window looking out at a starry night sky or a room with soft astronomical instruments (telescope, star charts), highlighting the educational, premium gift value.

### 3. Layout Mapping (English)
* **Brand Wordmark (Top-Right)**: Slanted **`EXCITAT` Speed Lightning Shelf** in the top-right corner, textured with dark carbon fiber and a solar-orange boundary glow.
* **Title (Top-Left)**: `SOLAR SYSTEM`
* **Subtitle**: `Electric Assembly Kit`
* **Core Numeric Badge (Click Catch)**: **1098 pcs** (enormous "1098", smaller "pcs" underneath inside a glowing orange circular badge in the top-left).
* **Secondary Feature Badges (Right column)**:
  1. `Auto Rotation` (🔄 Rotatable planetary gears)
  2. `Dual Mode` (🔌 Electric & manual drive)
  3. `STEM Gift` (🎓 Science & education focus)
* **Branded Trust Badge (Right Side)**:
  * An orange-bordered circular badge carrying: **`EXCITAT Guarantee`** (large "EXCITAT" in center, "Guarantee" below).
* **Bottom Info Bar**: `Realistic Gear Drive & Mechanical Function` (Muted orange-tinted text at the bottom).
