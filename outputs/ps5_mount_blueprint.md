# Universal E-commerce Blueprint: PS5 Wall Mount Kit

This blueprint documents the design layout planning and staging parameters for the EXCITAT PS5 Wall Mount Kit, following the **EXCITAT Universal Brand Shelf Framework**.

---

## Part 1: Product Analysis & Routing

### 1. Product Attributes
* **Product Name**: PS5 Wall Mount Kit (Slim & Pro compatible)
* **Features**: Dual Charger for controllers, Smart LED charging indicator, Customizable RGB lighting, Headphone hanger hook, Steel construction.
* **Compatibility**: PS5 Slim, PS5 Pro.
* **Color**: White mount & controllers.

### 2. Category Routing (Tech / Gaming Accessories)
* **Frame Family**: **Dark Neon / Tech Poster** (Gaming RGB theme).
* **Accent Palette**: Cyber Punk Glowing Cyan (`#00e5ff`) + Deep Charcoal Grey.
* **Staging Context**: Mounted on a gaming room wall with subtle RGB backlighting and cyber glow, highly relevant to real-life gaming setups.

### 3. Layout Mapping (English)
* **Brand Wordmark (Top-Right)**: Slanted **`EXCITAT` Speed Lightning Shelf** in the top-right corner, made of dark brushed metal texture with a cyan neon boundary light glow.
* **Title (Top-Left)**: `WALL MOUNT KIT`
* **Subtitle**: `For PS5 Slim & Pro`
* **Core Numeric Badge (Click Catch)**: **5-in-1** (Wall mount, dual charging dock, RGB, hook, indicators, inside a neon cyan badge in the top-left).
* **Secondary Feature Badges (Right column)**:
  1. `Dual Charger` (🎮 Controller charging)
  2. `RGB Light` (🌈 Customizable lighting)
  3. `Durable Steel` (🔩 Strong steel build)
* **Branded Trust Badge (Right Side)**:
  * A cyan circular badge carrying: **`1-Year Warranty`** (large "1-Year" in center, "Warranty" below). No generic shields.
* **Bottom Info Bar**: `Controller Charging Indicators & Hook` (Muted grey overlay text at the bottom).
